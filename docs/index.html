    How the score is computed
    • Weather score = average of three sub-scores: average max temperature, average sunshine, and total rain (inverted — less rain is better), over the next 7 days (Open-Meteo forecast).
    • CO₂ score: great-circle distance from the origin × 2 (round trip) × kg CO₂e per passenger-km, inverted — less CO₂ is better.
    • Every measure is normalized between the 27 capitals: the best gets 1, the worst gets 0. A score is relative to this list, not an absolute grade.
    • Final score = weather weight × weather score + CO₂ weight × CO₂ score. The published default is 50 / 50; the slider lets you test your own trade-off.
